# DemoRealvirtual — the sandbox project

This project ships with every realvirtual WEB installation. It is a **sandbox**:
open it, jog the drives, rearrange the layout, break it on purpose. Nothing here
is load-bearing for your own work.

## It is overwritten on every delivery

`demo-realvirtual/` is vendor-owned. Every realvirtual WEB delivery **replaces the
whole folder**, without merging and without asking. Anything you changed in it is
gone after the next update — that is the intended behaviour, not a bug, and it is
what keeps the demo identical on every channel.

## So: copy before you keep

If a demo scene is the starting point for something of yours, **copy it into your
own project first** (open the document, then *Save into project…*). A document in
your own project is yours, is never touched by a delivery, and is the only place
worth putting work you want to keep.
